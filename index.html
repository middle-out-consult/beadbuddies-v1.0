From a tiny spark to Bead Buddies

How Bead Buddies began

A little founder note for every crafter, collector, live-sale shopper, and bead lover who has helped this dream grow.

Follow on TikTok See the Goodies

People often ask why I love beads so much. The truth is, it all started when I decided to try a new craft. I quickly discovered that I wasn't exactly the crafty type, but I absolutely fell in love with the beads themselves! From the colours and shapes to the endless variety, I was hooked.

That little spark of an idea eventually grew into Bead Buddies. It took a lot longer than expected to get off the ground, but I'm so excited that it's finally here.

Bead Buddies is more than just a bead business—it's a community for people who love creating, collecting, and sharing their passion for crafting. I'd love your support as I continue to grow this little dream.

Please share my small bead business with your friends, family, and crafting family. Every share, like, follow, and purchase means the world to me.
